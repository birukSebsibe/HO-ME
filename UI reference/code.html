Pioneering Frontier Missions

Mobilizing the Horn of Africa Church from a Mission Field into a Mission Force.

The Horn of Africa is home to over 270 million people, including 43 highly unreached and unengaged people groups (UPGs). Historically, missions in the region relied heavily on external agencies and models unsuited to high-resistance contexts.

Since 2003, HO-ME has pioneered a paradigm shift: activating established evangelical congregations (specifically across the 4,000+ ECGBC churches and allied networks) to take sovereign responsibility for unreached frontiers at their doorsteps. Through inductive Discovery Bible Studies, Persons of Peace, and local economic resilience, we see generations of new believers birthed within their own cultural context.

4th Generation Multiplication

Moving past 1st generation converts to establish autonomous, self-multiplying house churches.

Kingdom Business & Self-Reliance

Replacing foreign dependency with local church stewardship, IGA micro-enterprises, and domestic giving.

Phase 1: The Engine Local Church Activation

Mobilize: Turning Churches Outward into Frontiers

Rather than creating detached mission organizations, HO-ME works with established congregations through our Churches of Peace initiative. We awaken leaders to see unreached peoples in their vicinity, shift mindsets from traditional institutional maintenance to frontier mission, and prepare candidates for apostolic sending.

Key Methodologies:

  • "Church of Peace" mobilization seminars conducted in local synods and zones.
  • Identification, screening, and sending candidate church planters directly supported by mother churches.
  • Frontier prayer networks mobilizing continuous intercession for unreached people clusters.

Faithful Roots & Milestones

Our 20+ Year Historical Journey

From a small prayer meeting in Addis Ababa to a cross-regional catalytic movement impacting thousands of unreached communities.

2003 — HOAM Founded

Horn of Africa Mission Born

2006 — The DMM Paradigm Shift

2012–2015 — Consolidation

2021–2025 — M2025 Campaign

Surpassing 23,000 House Churches
